WHAT EVERY ORBITER CARRIES

INSIDE THE SPACECRAFT

The same four systems decide whether a mission brings its science home. Pick one to highlight it on the spacecraft.

01 · Science instrumentsSCIENCE
Cameras, spectrometers, altimeters and sounders, modelled on OSIRIS-APEX, LRO, Aura and MRO instruments.
02 · Recorder and flight computerDATA
Stores data between contacts with Earth. When the recorder is full, new data is lost.
03 · Radio and high-gain antennaCOMMS
Sends data home only while a ground station or the Deep Space Network can hear it.
04 · Solar arrays and batteryPOWER
Arrays power the craft in sunlight; the battery carries it through eclipse.
