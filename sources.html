    Cosmic Explorer observations

      Images are resized for the web. Infrared and radio data are mapped to visible colors by the original teams. ESA/Webb images are credited under the ESA/Webb usage terms. The illustrated Earth and Moon globes use the maps linked above.

      Music

      The background music is generated live in the browser by our own code. No recordings are used.

      Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 by team NEXUS//04. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA. No NASA logos are used. Earth, Moon and Mars use real global maps. The shape of Apophis is illustrative, because no close-up map of it exists yet. It is drawn as a rocky rubble pile, like the asteroids Itokawa and Bennu that spacecraft have visited.
